THE INVESTMENT THESIS

Strategy creation is getting cheaper. Capital judgement is not.

Systematic teams can create, test and approve more strategies than ever. The difficult institutional workflow is deciding which strategies still deserve capital as evidence, correlations and market conditions change.

Prospect is being built to own that decision layer.

BUSINESS MODEL

Institutional B2B software at a high-value decision layer.

The initial commercial model is enterprise/platform software. Expansion comes from more strategies, more teams and deeper portfolio, regime, risk and allocation workflows.

Longer-term capital-linked economics may be possible where appropriate, but they are not required for the initial software model.
