Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA and a useful experience across devices and input methods.

Using the site

Navigation and forms support keyboards, visible focus and labelled controls. The site respects reduced-motion preferences: animation and smooth scrolling switch off and all content shows immediately. Product screenshots have text descriptions and are not required to complete signup.

Feedback

If something prevents you from accessing the site, email the founder with the page and issue. We welcome reports and do not claim independent accessibility certification.
