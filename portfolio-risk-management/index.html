Prospect / Research & understanding

See risk across strategies.

Portfolio risk analysis looks beyond individual strategies to the exposures they share.

Correlation is only a starting point

Strategies that look different may respond similarly under stress. Historical correlations can shift and do not describe every shared dependency.

Concentration can hide in plain sight

Consider common markets, factors, time horizons and liquidity. Adding more strategy names does not necessarily create diversification.

A connected research workflow

Prospect’s planned portfolio intelligence aims to help traders investigate concentrations and strategy interactions. It does not guarantee lower risk or investment success.
