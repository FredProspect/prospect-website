Can I use Prospect today?

Prospect is pre-launch. The screens on this page are prototype previews. Join the waitlist to receive launch and early-access information; registration does not guarantee beta access or a launch date.

Will Prospect trade for me?

No. Prospect is being developed for strategy research and trading intelligence. This website does not offer autonomous trading, brokerage execution or fund management.

Do I need to know how to code?

The AI-assisted builder aims to make strategy development accessible through natural language. It is still in development; supported workflows and their limitations will be explained at launch.

Does backtesting prove a strategy will work?

No. Historical tests depend on data quality, costs and assumptions. Overfitting and changing market conditions can make results misleading. Trading can result in losses.

What do I get by joining the waitlist?

Emails about Prospect’s launch and early access, and nothing else. We never ask for trading credentials, holdings or financial information, and you can withdraw consent at any time.
