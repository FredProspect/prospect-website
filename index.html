THE THESIS

More strategies create a harder capital-allocation problem.

Systematic investment teams can research and deploy increasingly sophisticated strategies. The harder problem begins after a strategy exists: does it still deserve capital, how much, and what changes when the evidence weakens?

Backtests age. Correlations move. Drawdowns deepen. Market regimes change. Prospect is being built to make those decisions continuous, portfolio-aware and traceable rather than fragmented across disconnected tools.

THE PRODUCT

A decision layer above approved strategies.

Prospect connects strategy evidence, changing market context, portfolio risk and capital allocation in one operating loop.

01

Strategy intelligence

Bring approved strategies into a common evidence framework so research quality, live behaviour and recent changes can be compared consistently.

02

Behaviour-change detection

Identify when a strategy begins behaving materially differently from its tested or recent operating range.

03

Regime-aware scoring

Combine longer-term evidence with volatility, drawdown, market context and regime fit into a changing view of strategy reliability.

04

Portfolio risk

Evaluate exposure, concentration, correlation and drawdown at portfolio level rather than strategy by strategy.

05

Adaptive allocation

Translate strategy scores and portfolio constraints into bounded, explainable capital-allocation proposals.

06

Decision traceability

Record what the system saw, why a score changed, which rule applied and how the portfolio response followed.

INITIAL WEDGE

Know when strategy behaviour changes — and what the portfolio should do next.

The first product focus is deliberately narrow: monitor the changing reliability of systematic strategies, place that change in portfolio context, and make the capital response clearer.

That wedge can expand into the broader Prospect operating system: research standards, portfolio risk, allocation, decision logging and controlled execution.

Start narrow. Build the decision layer. Expand with evidence.

INSTITUTIONAL BY DESIGN

AI where judgement changes. Hard rules where capital is at risk.

Prospect is designed so model output can inform strategy scores and allocation proposals without overriding explicit portfolio controls. The product should remain understandable, reviewable and recoverable as automation increases.

01Bounded outputs

Models propose within fixed ranges and eligibility rules.

02Hard risk controls

Exposure, drawdown and concentration limits remain explicit.

03Human approval

High-impact actions retain operator oversight during early deployment.

04Audit trail

Data, model versions, allocation changes and interventions are logged.

WHY NOW

Investment workflows are moving from AI-assisted research toward AI-assisted decisions.

01

More evidence can be processed continuously

Performance, risk, market context and portfolio relationships can be evaluated together rather than as isolated snapshots.

02

The decision loop remains fragmented

Research, risk and allocation often live in separate systems, leaving the portfolio decision itself under-connected.

03

Trust requires traceability

For serious capital, intelligence is useful only when inputs, rationale, limits and resulting actions remain visible.

BUSINESS MODEL

Institutional software first. Capital-linked economics over time.

Prospect is being designed for systematic hedge funds, quant teams, asset managers and other professional investment organisations.

The commercial path begins with platform contracts and high-value institutional workflows. As Prospect becomes embedded in portfolio decisions, capital-linked economics can become an additional layer rather than the starting dependency.
