Questions

What happens when I join?

You’ll get launch and early-access news by email. You can answer a few optional questions about how you trade, which helps us decide who to invite to the first beta groups. Joining doesn’t guarantee beta access or a launch date.

Can I use Prospect today?

Not yet. Prospect is pre-launch and the screens on this page are prototypes or synthetic examples.

Will Prospect trade for me?

No. Prospect is for strategy research and understanding. It doesn’t execute trades, manage funds or move your money.

Do I need to code?

No. You describe ideas in plain English. Supported markets and workflows will be explained at launch.

Does a good backtest mean a strategy will work?

No. Historical tests depend on data, costs and assumptions, and markets change. Trading can result in losses.
