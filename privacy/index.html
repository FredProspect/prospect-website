Privacy notice

How Prospect Partnership Ltd handles your information when you join the waitlist or contact us. Updated 16 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Prospect Partnership Ltd, Company No. 15277481, England & Wales is the controller. Contact founder@prospectpartnership.co.uk about your data.

Waitlist information

We collect your email address, signup time, consent version and time, and a limited source label if you arrive from a campaign. We use your consent to send relevant launch and early-access emails. We do not ask for trading credentials, holdings or financial information.

Security and abuse prevention

Our server uses a keyed hash of your network address to limit attempts. This record expires after 15 minutes and is removed on subsequent requests. Hosting providers may process technical logs. We do not put your email into analytics or application logs.

Your choices

Joining is voluntary. Withdraw consent or request deletion at any time by emailing the founder. We do not sell your details. We will not send waitlist emails before an authorised communication process is configured.

Storage and retention

Waitlist records are stored in a restricted database accessible only to authorised administrators and hosting providers. We retain them while providing the requested launch updates, review them at least annually, and remove them when no longer needed or when you withdraw consent, subject to legal obligations. Security records have the shorter retention described above.

Service providers and international transfers

The public website uses GitHub Pages, and waitlist collection uses a dedicated Railway service with private persistent storage. These hosting providers, and any authorised email provider, may process information on our behalf. Where processing outside the UK requires safeguards, appropriate transfer arrangements must be in place. Contact us for current provider details.

Your rights

You may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data and to object to processing. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We update this notice when our processing changes.
