What we collect

This website does not contain account registration, payment or lead-capture forms. Information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, email, document storage and business operations. We do not sell personal information. We do not use this website for behavioural advertising.

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including legitimate business record-keeping, security and legal requirements. We review information when it is no longer needed.

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict your personal information, object to certain processing, request portability and complain about how your data is used. You can contact us at founder@prospectpartnership.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

We will update this notice if the way we collect or use personal information changes materially.
