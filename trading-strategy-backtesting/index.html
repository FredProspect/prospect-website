Prospect / Research & understanding

Test the idea. Question the result.

Historical strategy testing can inform research, but it cannot tell you what happens next.

Account for real-world friction

Consider transaction costs, spreads, slippage, liquidity and data quality. A historical price is not a guarantee you could have traded at that price.

Watch for overfitting

Repeatedly tuning a strategy to the same history can make noise look like an edge. Keep separate evaluation periods, consider out-of-sample checks and document the decisions made along the way.

Keep the limitations visible

Markets change. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Prospect is developing AI-assisted research tools; this website does not run backtests or show performance results.
