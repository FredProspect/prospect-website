Prospect / Research & understanding

Understand when behaviour changes.

Strategy monitoring means looking for evidence that deserves review, rather than assuming yesterday’s pattern will persist.

Define what you expect

Record the strategy thesis, dependencies and expected behaviour before interpreting changes. Without context, a deviation can be hard to distinguish from ordinary variation.

Investigate, don’t automate the conclusion

Changes in drawdowns, turnover, exposures or market conditions may warrant investigation. Alerts cannot prove that an edge has disappeared or tell you what to trade.

Prospect’s direction

Prospect is being built to connect strategy evidence, changing behaviour and portfolio context for independent traders. Monitoring functionality is still in development.
