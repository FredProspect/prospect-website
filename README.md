# Prospect

[Prospect](https://prospectpartnership.co.uk/) is building AI-native portfolio intelligence and decision infrastructure for systematic investment teams — connecting strategy monitoring, portfolio risk and explainable capital allocation.

**Legal company:** Prospect Partnership Ltd · Company No. 15277481 · England & Wales

**Website:** https://prospectpartnership.co.uk/

Prospect is early-stage and building V1. The initial product is intended as analytics and decision support for systematic investment teams; customers retain discretion over capital deployment.
