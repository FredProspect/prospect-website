The product

Prospect: AI-assisted trading research for independent traders

Prospect is being built so independent traders can do the kind of strategy research that used to need a quant team: describe an idea, get clear rules, test them honestly, watch them live and see risk across everything you run.

On this page

  1. Who it’s for
  2. One connected workflow
  3. What Prospect will and won’t do
  4. Where it is today

Who it’s for

Retail traders, prop traders and systematic thinkers who have ideas but not a research department. You don’t need to write code. You do need to want evidence over hype.

One connected workflow

  1. Build: describe a strategy in plain English and Prospect drafts the full rules for you to review. Learn more.
  2. Research: backtest with costs, slippage and held-back data. Learn more.
  3. Understand: see whether there are enough trades, which conditions results depend on and how fragile they are.
  4. Monitor: get flagged when live behaviour drifts from what you expected. Learn more.
  5. Manage: see correlation, concentration and overlap across your whole portfolio. Learn more.

What Prospect will and won’t do

  • It shows rules you can read and edit, not black-box signals.
  • It shows evidence with its limitations, not backtests sold as proof.
  • It looks at risk across your whole portfolio, not one trade at a time.
  • It assists your decisions. It does not execute trades, manage money or promise returns.
