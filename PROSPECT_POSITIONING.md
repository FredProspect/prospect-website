# Prospect positioning doctrine

Prospect is building the **AI-native intelligence and decision layer between systematic strategies and capital**.

**Master line:** Intelligence for the portfolio.

## Product architecture

Prospect connects five layers:

1. **Strategy understanding** — strategy identity, thesis, family, dependencies and evidence provenance.
2. **Strategy intelligence** — strategy-specific evidence, behavioural fingerprints, changing reliability and uncertainty.
3. **Regime intelligence** — market context and strategy/regime compatibility.
4. **Portfolio intelligence** — correlation, concentration, common exposures, diversification and cross-strategy interactions.
5. **Capital-allocation intelligence** — constraint-aware, explainable allocation scenarios for human review.

## Non-negotiable invariant

**Monitoring is a primitive, never the product.**

Strategy reliability, drift detection, anomaly detection, STABLE/WATCH/REVIEW states and review objects are executable capabilities inside the wider Prospect intelligence layer. They must never redefine Prospect as a generic monitoring or reliability platform.

The portfolio is the first-class object. The workflow ends in a capital decision.

## Current boundary

Prospect is currently being built as institutional decision-support software. It supplies evidence, intelligence and explainable allocation scenarios while customers retain control of capital.

## Website rule

Any page can discuss a specific sub-problem such as strategy monitoring, drift, portfolio risk or capital allocation. But shared company framing must always return to the full Prospect architecture and the portfolio-level decision layer.

## Preferred language

- “Intelligence for the portfolio.”
- “Prospect is building the AI-native intelligence and decision layer between systematic strategies and capital.”
- “AI-native intelligence and decision infrastructure for systematic investment firms.”

## Drift test

Before changing the website, ask:

- Does this treat the portfolio as a first-class object?
- Does it strengthen strategy, regime, portfolio or allocation intelligence?
- Is monitoring positioned as a primitive rather than the company?
- Does the story connect evidence to a capital decision?

If not, reframe the work.
