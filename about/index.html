ABOUT PROSPECT

Intelligence for the portfolio.

Prospect is building the AI-native intelligence and decision layer between systematic strategies and capital.

COMPANY

Prospect is being built around one question.

Which strategies deserve capital now, how much, and why?

Prospect is a UK company building the AI-native intelligence and decision layer between systematic strategies and capital for professional investment firms.

The company is starting with strategy reliability and change detection while building toward the full portfolio-intelligence architecture.

FOUNDER

Founder-led from systematic research into portfolio intelligence.

FRED BOXER

Founder & CEO

Prospect grew from direct work with systematic strategy research, automation and risk. The core insight was that once multiple strategies exist, the harder problem becomes deciding which evidence deserves attention, how strategies interact and where capital should move as evidence changes.
