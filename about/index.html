About Prospect

Prospect is a UK startup building AI-assisted trading research tools for independent traders. It was founded by Fred Boxer, a systematic trader who wanted better tools for people without a research team behind them.

Why Prospect exists

Fred founded Prospect after independently building and running systematic trading systems with AI-assisted development. The tools that make careful research possible (precise rules, honest testing, monitoring and portfolio risk) were either locked inside institutions or required serious programming. Meanwhile, retail traders were being sold signals, bots and screenshots.

Prospect’s ambition is simple: make sophisticated strategy research accessible to independent traders, built around process, evidence and risk rather than hype.

Where we are

Prospect is pre-launch and in active development. Product screens on this site are prototypes, and there is no confirmed public launch date. The waitlist is open, and some members will be selected as beta testers.
