Prospect / Research & understanding

From trading idea to research plan.

What to look for in an AI trading strategy builder, and what Prospect is working towards.

Make the rules explicit

An idea is a starting point. Define the market, timing, entry and exit rules, position sizing and conditions that would invalidate the thesis. AI suggestions still need human review.

No-code does not mean no judgement

Natural language can reduce technical barriers. It does not establish that an idea is sensible or profitable. Inspect assumptions and verify any generated rules before testing.

Prospect’s direction

The planned builder connects idea development to research and risk intelligence. See the prototype; AI drafting is not connected in the captured version.
