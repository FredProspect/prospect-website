Prospect does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers or other non-essential cookies on this website.

Current setup

The site is designed as a simple static website. Prospect does not intentionally store non-essential cookies or similar tracking identifiers on your device. Because we do not currently set non-essential cookies, there is no consent banner to accept or reject.

Technical delivery

Website infrastructure providers may receive standard request information needed to serve and secure the site, such as IP address, user agent, timestamps and request logs. This is different from Prospect placing an analytics or advertising cookie on your device.

External links

The site contains links that can open your email application or take you to third-party websites such as LinkedIn. Those services have their own privacy and cookie practices once you choose to visit them.

If this changes

If Prospect later introduces non-essential cookies, analytics or similar technologies that require consent, we will update this policy and add an appropriate consent control before those technologies are activated.
